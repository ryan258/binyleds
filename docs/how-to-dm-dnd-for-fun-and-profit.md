# Sustainable preparation and facilitation

This guide applies to personal and professional tables. Paid delivery is an
optional context. The first constraint is the operator’s available energy and
preferred way of working; more sessions are not automatically a better outcome.

## Reuse structure while listening to the group

Keep a small library of scenes with an objective, two or more approaches, clues
that do not depend on one successful roll, and consequences that keep play moving.
Change motives, details, and choices to suit the group. Reusing your own structure
can reduce preparation; it does not establish permission to reuse someone else’s
published game material.

The [Lantern Bay pack](../examples/lantern-bay/README.md) demonstrates this with
three locations, three NPC motives, redundant clues, adjustable pacing, and
several endings. It includes a minimum-prep path and an optional enrichment path.
Run `npm run session` from the repository root to make a fresh private working
copy of the example under `.local/sessions/` before adapting it.

## Budget effort before promising detail

List must-have preparation, optional atmosphere, and the point at which work
stops. Prefer a readable handout and clear choices to materials that create more
handling or explanation. Use dictation, reusable snippets, numbered actions, and
short checklists. Keep private notes in a private workspace, never in the public
Hugo content directory.

Allow breaks, a fallback if technology fails, and enough schedule space around
the session. Decide with the actual group how to signal a pause or adjust tone.
Avoid guarantees about access arrangements until the venue or platform has been
confirmed.

## Review the session without turning it into a sales funnel

Ask what worked, what was difficult, and whether anything needs follow-up. A new
session is an invitation to discuss, never the default use of an inquiry address.
For paid sessions, compare actual effort and expense with the agreed scope using
the [pricing worksheet](pricing.md). For personal play, judge the session by its
value to the group and its cost in energy.

No general rule here forbids discounts, collaboration, hiring, rest, or stopping.
Choose according to the specific situation and the operator’s own priorities.
