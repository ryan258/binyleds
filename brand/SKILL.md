# Binyled’s Storyscape

Use this project-local skill when writing, reviewing, or extending the website,
consultation materials, proposals, social copy, or guest communications.

## Project rules

1. Lead with what the host can give their guests and the burden removed.
2. Demonstrate care with specific preparation, guidance, and guest consideration.
3. Use fantasy as a measured payoff after practical clarity.
4. Speak as one practitioner using “I”; use “you” to keep the host central.
5. Never invent biography, credentials, inclusions, testimonials, pricing,
   capacity, service area, policies, or affiliation claims.

## Voice profiles

- **Gracious private host:** attentive, personal, calm; never pretentious.
- **Master storyteller:** evocative, intriguing, emotionally intelligent; never
  overwritten.
- **Discreet concierge:** prepared, clear, reassuring; never boastful or
  transactional.

Use the blend **40% gracious private host / 35% master storyteller / 25%
discreet concierge**.

## Format

1. Practical reassurance
2. Specific act of care
3. Imaginative payoff
4. Calm consultation invitation

## Output template

```text
[Outcome-led headline]

[What the host wants and what is removed from their shoulders.]

[Concrete preparation or guidance that makes the promise credible.]

[One restrained imaginative image that returns agency to the guests.]

[Specific consultation invitation.]
```

## What not to do

- Do not say “luxury D&D,” “elite Dungeon Master,” or “ultimate game night.”
- Do not use quest jokes in conversion copy.
- Do not make wealth or corporate status explicit.
- Do not use fake scarcity.
- Do not invent testimonials or credentials.
- Do not make beginners sound like a burden.
- Do not let fantasy ornament obscure the practical experience.
