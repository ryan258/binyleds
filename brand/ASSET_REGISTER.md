### Final production prompt

Create one cohesive, premium editorial landscape social card for Binyled’s
Storyscape, a bespoke in-home tabletop experience brand. Show an intimate
private-library tabletop at evening with dark forest-green leather or felt, a
restrained parchment map, one elegant metal twenty-sided die, a few handwritten
story notes, and warm antique-brass details. Reserve the left side for calm,
legible typography and arrange the still life on the right. Use the exact text
“Binyled’s Storyscape,” “You host the people. I host the world.” and “Bespoke
in-home tabletop experiences.” Use an elegant old-style serif and precise modern
sans serif in obsidian, parchment, verdigris, bright-mint accent, antique gold,
and deep forest. Keep the result intimate, editorial, and quietly prepared.
Do not invent a dragon, shield, crest, or logo. Avoid generic AI fantasy,
glowing magic, castles, weapons, cosplay, medieval borders, clutter, neon,
esports graphics, wealth signaling, watermarks, misspellings, or any other text.

## Utility seal

- **File:** `static/images/utility-seal.png`
- **Purpose:** Favicon source and digital utility monogram
- **Source:** Deterministically generated by `scripts/generate_favicons.py`
- **Status:** Utility-only; not the master logo

The supplied brand source refers to an existing dragon-and-shield crest but did
not include the approved artwork. The BS seal must not be represented as a
replacement. When the approved crest becomes available, preserve its
recognizable dragon and shield rather than inventing a new symbol.

## Favicon family

- `static/favicon.ico`
- `static/favicon-16x16.png`
- `static/favicon-32x32.png`

These are derived from the utility seal and may be regenerated with:

```sh
python3 scripts/generate_favicons.py
```

## Typography assets

- **Cormorant Garamond Variable:** display and ceremonial typography
- **Manrope Variable:** body, navigation, forms, and operating information

The WOFF2 files are self-hosted under `static/fonts/`. Both families use the SIL
Open Font License; license texts are stored in `LICENSES/`.
