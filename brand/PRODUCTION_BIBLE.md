# Binyled’s Storyscape production bible

## What is it

Binyled’s Storyscape is a bespoke in-home tabletop roleplaying experience. A
private host gathers the people; the Storyscape practitioner prepares and guides
the world. The first public product is **The Private Storyscape**, a single-evening
commission created around the guests.

## Setting

The brand lives at the intersection of a private library, an intimate dinner,
and a living fantasy world. It belongs in a discerning client's home: composed,
warm, quietly atmospheric, and never theatrical for its own sake.

## Tone

**Gracious. Assured. Imaginative.** The voice welcomes like a thoughtful host,
guides like a prepared practitioner, and reveals fantasy only after the practical
promise is clear.

## Format rules

1. Lead with the host's outcome before describing the game.
2. Show preparation through concrete actions rather than prestige adjectives.
3. Reassure beginners without speaking down to them.
4. Keep the host and guests at the center; the game master is the guide.
5. Move from practical reassurance to a specific act of care, then to an
   imaginative payoff.
6. End with a calm invitation to a private consultation.

## What it is not

- Not a children's party-entertainment brand.
- Not a game-store, esports, or convention aesthetic.
- Not generic “luxury D&D” copy dressed in fantasy adjectives.
- Not a promise of credentials, testimonials, pricing, or inclusions that have
  not been verified.

## Voice profiles

### The gracious private host

- **Core want:** Make every guest feel expected and comfortable.
- **Default behavior:** Anticipates friction and explains what happens next.
- **Voice example:** “Every adventure begins with a conversation about your
  guests, your home, and the evening you want to create.”
- **Must never:** Become aristocratic, ceremonial, or cold.

### The master storyteller

- **Core want:** Give each guest a meaningful place in a shared story.
- **Default behavior:** Uses one vivid image, then returns the choice to the
  table.
- **Voice example:** “By evening's end, one beautifully reckless decision may
  have changed the whole kingdom.”
- **Must never:** Drift into generic epic monologues or destiny language.

### The discreet concierge

- **Core want:** Remove the host's burden without drawing attention to the work.
- **Default behavior:** Names what is prepared, guided, and handled.
- **Voice example:** “From the first introduction to the final decision, I guide
  the pace and keep every guest involved.”
- **Must never:** Boast, dominate, or make rules mastery the product.

## Craft rules

- **Rule 1:** The host stays at the table.
- **Rule 2:** Never claim premium when specific care can demonstrate it.
- **Rule 3:** Every page earns its invitation before presenting it.
- **Rule 4:** The emotional product is confidence that the guests will have an
  exceptional evening.
- **Rule 5:** Fantasy supports hospitality; it never overwhelms it.
- **Rule 6:** Unknown business facts stay unknown until the owner confirms them.

## Production window

The site is a static Hugo project. New copy should be drafted against the brand
guide, checked with `npm run check`, and published through the configured host.
Small content updates should remain possible without redesigning templates.
