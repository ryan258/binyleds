# Storyscape consultation and client communications

November 2026 · Editable drafts for the practitioner

Use these in an existing inquiry or agreed engagement. For local rehearsal, use the [fictional Lantern Bay pack](../../examples/lantern-bay/README.md). For active inquiries, use the approved contact channel and confirm the actual arrangements before promising a date, price, or inclusion. Replace bracketed fields and read the message once before sending. When the person has already supplied a detail, carry it forward.

## Reply and qualify

**Subject** — Your private Storyscape inquiry

> Hi [first name],
>
> Thank you for telling me about [occasion or short detail they shared]. I have [date or timing] and [general location] in mind. Before I suggest an evening, could you tell me [one missing detail that determines fit]? A rough answer is fine.
>
> Once I know what would suit the group, I can outline the evening and what it would cost. Your request opens the conversation. We’ll confirm the date and scope directly before you make a commitment.
>
> [Practitioner name]

Ask one fit question at a time. Start with the item most likely to change the answer. When someone writes by email without describing the group’s experience, offer a quick choice. “Which best describes the table? A, mostly new. B, a mix. C, mostly experienced. A letter or your own answer is fine.”

The active form asks for the host’s name, email, general location, estimated guest count, tabletop experience, a short description of the gathering, and consent to use those details for the reply. Date, phone, occasion, desired feeling, and comfort or access notes are optional. “Not sure yet” is available for tabletop experience. [Consultation form](../../layouts/consultation/single.html) · [Intake fields](../../data/intake.json)

Use the private consultation to confirm the plan and practical fit.

- The gathering, its general location, date options, and expected guest count.
- The group’s experience and desired tone. A few plain-language examples can help.
- Space, timing, breaks, content boundaries, and ways to make participation easier. Ask for practical needs and only the detail needed to plan.
- Who can agree to the scope, how they prefer to communicate, and what they should expect next.

For the optional Cloudflare inbox, the [runbook](../../docs/site-operations.md) documents short commands such as `npm run inbox -- i` to list and `npm run inbox -- r 1` to read a current selection. Update contact status after an actual reply and refresh before acting on an old selector. A notification to the operator and a response to the host are separate steps. Store the correspondence and agreed scope in the approved private workflow.

## Send a proposed scope

**Subject** — A proposed Storyscape for [occasion]

> Hi [first name],
>
> For [occasion], I suggest [brief tone and premise]. Guests will begin with [simple opening action], and I’ll guide the evening as they make their choices. Here’s the plan we discussed.
>
> **Date and place** — [date, start and end, general location, agreed group size].  
> **Preparation and guidance** — [actual work and deliverables included].  
> **Materials and setup** — [what I bring, what you provide, and setup and departure timing].  
> **Participation** — [agreed breaks, explanations, and practical arrangements in language the host expects to see].  
> **Quote** — [total, currency, what it covers, and any applicable fees or taxes].  
> **Terms and confirmation** — [owner-approved payment, cancellation and changes, and exact step that confirms the booking].
>
> If the plan suits your gathering, [one specific reply or confirmation action]. If something needs to change, tell me which part and I’ll confirm the revised scope before we settle the date.
>
> [Practitioner name]

Use [the pricing worksheet](../../docs/pricing.md) and [sample scope](../../examples/lantern-bay/02-scope.md) to turn the notes into a real quote. Write the exact list of included materials. Give the host the full approved terms and keep a versioned copy of what they accepted. Use plain, confirmable language when negotiating a scope change.

## Close respectfully when a gathering is outside scope

**Subject** — About your Storyscape request

> Hi [first name],
>
> Thank you for sharing the plan for [occasion]. I’m unable to take on the gathering as described because [brief and accurate practical reason]. If [one workable adjustment] would suit you, I’m happy to discuss that. I hope the evening comes together well.
>
> [Practitioner name]

Omit the adjustment sentence when there is no viable option. Offer a referral when it is genuinely useful and permitted. Let the host choose whether to contact another provider.

## Confirm the agreed evening

**Subject** — Confirmed details for [occasion]

> Hi [first name],
>
> I’ve recorded your agreement to [scope version/date]. Your Storyscape is confirmed for [date and start/end time] at [agreed location]. I’ll arrive at [agreed setup time] and bring [agreed materials]. Please have [host’s actual setup responsibilities] ready. If anything changes, reach me through [agreed contact method] so we can check the plan together.
>
> You can forward the guest note below. I’m looking forward to seeing what the table chooses.
>
> [Practitioner name]

Send the actual home address or other private logistics only through the agreed channel to the people who need them. If the chosen terms require a separate booking step, send this confirmation after that step is complete.

## Guest note the host can forward

> We’re getting together for a guided tabletop adventure on [day and time]. A game master will describe the opening scene, and we’ll decide what our characters do next. You can ask a question, talk through a choice with someone, or describe what your character tries. Rules will be explained as they come up.
>
> Please bring [agreed details, or “just yourself” if confirmed]. Reply privately to [host or agreed contact] if there’s anything that would help you join in comfortably. [Agreed logistics or private link]

Use alongside the [guest invitation](../collateral/guest-invitation.pdf) or send as plain text. Keep the date, location, and contact easy to find. Forward the agreed guest-facing details only.

## Follow up after a real evening

**Subject** — Thank you for the evening

> Hi [first name],
>
> Thank you for bringing the group together. [One accurate observation about what the table chose, if useful.] If you’d like to tell me one thing to keep or change, a short reply is plenty. A, pace. B, explanations and participation. C, practical arrangements. Your own words are welcome, and a reply is optional.
>
> [Practitioner name]

An inquiry or a session is permission to discuss that work. Ask separately for permission before using a quote, photo, or case study. Show the exact material, attribution, and intended placement and record the reply. Keep practical needs, personal details, and home locations out of public material. The [communications plan](../../docs/marketing-campaign.md) covers continued contact and opt-in.
