## What the facilitator can infer

A short, welcoming introduction and a self-contained story are sensible starting
points. Lantern Bay offers investigation, negotiation, and a shared final choice.
Do not infer a diagnosis, venue accessibility, platform preference, availability,
fee, or consent to share the group’s story.

## One useful next question

“Which setting would work best: A, around a table; B, online with voice and text;
or C, still deciding?” Accept a letter or any short answer. Resolve practical
questions one at a time instead of sending the host a large questionnaire.
