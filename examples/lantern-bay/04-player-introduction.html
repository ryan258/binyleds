Fictional sample session · System-neutral · No prior reading required

Welcome to Lantern Bay

Tonight the lighthouse shines inland. Its beam reveals a silver path to an orchard nobody remembers planting. A warm light waits between the trees. What would you like to find out?

How to play

Say what your character wants to do. Ask questions whenever you want. You may speak as yourself, speak as the character, type, point to an option, or ask someone to read your choice. You never have to perform a voice.

The guide describes what happens and makes the possible costs clear. You choose what to try. You can change your mind when new information appears.

Choose a role, or make your own

  • A — The Listener. People tell you what they need. You carry a notebook.
  • B — The Maker. You understand how things fit together. You carry simple tools.
  • C — The Wanderer. You notice paths and changes in the landscape. You carry a lantern.
  • D — The Neighbor. You care about the town and know its routines. You carry a shared meal.

Every role can investigate, help, talk, or try an unexpected idea. No role owns the only solution. Choose a name, or let the guide offer one.

Your first choice

A: Visit the lighthouse. B: Ask the market crowd what changed. C: Follow the silver path. A different idea is welcome.

Pause, skip, or change. Anyone may use these words or the agreed signal at any time, without explaining why. You may pass on a scene, step away, or take a break. The story can wait.

Before we begin

This story suggests darkness, a mild storm, and disagreement about an old promise. Tell the guide privately or at the table if you want any of these changed. No combat or graphic violence is required.

We will finish with a choice about the orchard and a short chance to say what worked, what to change, or simply “pass.”
