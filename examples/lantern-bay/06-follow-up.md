# Follow-up draft — not sent

Use only after a real session, with the actual recipient and agreed purpose.
This example does not establish permission for ongoing updates or publication.

**Subject:** After our Lantern Bay session

Thank you for sharing the table. I appreciated the choices the group brought to
the orchard. I’d welcome a brief note about anything you would keep or change in
the way the session worked. A letter is enough: A, the pace; B, explanations and
participation; C, practical arrangements; or your own short answer. No reply is required.

If there is a private concern or a detail you would like corrected or removed
from the inquiry record, you can raise it in this conversation.

Before sending, replace this draft’s general appreciation with an accurate
observation, or leave it simple. Do not invent a compliment, event detail, or
player reaction. Ask separately about a future session, quotation, or photograph
only when that conversation is welcome. Do not enroll anyone in updates.
