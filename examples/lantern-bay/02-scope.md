# Draft scope: Lantern Bay

**Status:** synthetic proposal for review, not an agreement or reservation.

## Proposed experience

A complete, system-neutral mystery in which a lighthouse reveals a forgotten
orchard and the group decides how its promise to the town should be renewed.
The example assumes four participants and a 90-minute plan including a short
introduction, flexible break, final choice, and debrief. Adjust before agreement.

## Included in this example

- Facilitated introduction, consent check, and explanation of available choices.
- Four optional roles with no required statistics or prior reading.
- Three explorable locations, three NPC motives, redundant clues, and open endings.
- A plain-language player handout and facilitator prep sheet.
- A short debrief and optional, individually approved follow-up message.

Optional music, maps, miniatures, printed keepsakes, and additional preparation
are outside this base example. Do not treat attractive website illustrations as
included equipment. A remote version needs an agreed platform and reconnect plan;
an in-person version needs confirmed seating, lighting, access, and break arrangements.

## Before anything is accepted

Record the actual participants/count, date, start/end window, location or platform,
access arrangements, tone and boundaries, scope changes, and who confirms the plan.
If this becomes paid work, add an owner-approved quote and terms before asking
for a commitment. No price, deposit, cancellation rule, or legal term is supplied
by this synthetic proposal. The original inquiry creates no payment obligation.
