## Your gathering shapes the adventure

The Private Storyscape is one guided evening at your table. Tell me what
brings the group together, how familiar they are with tabletop games, and what
they’d enjoy. A table of curious first-time players might start with a gentle
mystery. An experienced group might want room to follow a lead of their own.

{{< callout title="The promise" tone="signature" >}}
You invite the guests. The world arrives prepared.
{{< /callout >}}

## What I can prepare

I’ll confirm what’s included in your evening before you decide. Depending on
your group and the agreed story, that may include:

- A focused adventure created for the group
- Ready-to-play characters or help making your own
- A welcoming introduction for first-time players
- Rules guidance as questions come up
- A pace that gives guests room to ask questions and make choices
- Maps, music, miniatures, and selected physical details where useful
- A self-contained story with a clear opening and ending

{{< callout title="How an evening might begin" >}}
A lighthouse is shining inland. Its beam reveals a path no one remembers
building. Do you follow the light, or find out who lit it?
{{< /callout >}}

## What guests experience

I describe a scene and ask what your characters do. You can speak in character
or describe your choice in everyday words. I explain the rules as they come up
and give people time to ask a question or pass a turn. The choices you make
together change what happens next.

## What the host does

Bring the people, share what would help them settle in, and tell me what the
room is like. I’ll confirm the details with you before the evening. When play
begins, you can join your guests while I guide the story.
