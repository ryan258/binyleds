## 1. Tell me about your gathering

Start with three things: a possible date, your area, and a rough guest
count. Then tell me what brings these people together, what they enjoy, and
how much tabletop gaming they’ve done.

One story idea helps if you have it. Friends new to games often love a gentle
mystery. Escape-room fans tend to chase a trail of clues. A group that already
plays might want a familiar world and a fresh dilemma.

Tone, content to leave out, and anything that would help someone take part can
all come up here, privately. The [host’s checklist]({{< relref "host-guide" >}})
lists what’s worth having ready.

An inquiry is only a conversation. No reservation, no payment.

## 2. Get a clear plan and quote

I reply with what fits your group, your room, and your schedule: timing,
what’s included, and the full price. If the date or format won’t work, I’ll
tell you plainly.

Once you say yes, I write the story, an opening choice anyone can grasp in
seconds, and any characters or materials we agreed on.

## 3. Pull up a chair

I set the opening scene, ask what your characters do, and explain rules only
when they matter. You play alongside your guests. Questions and breaks are
always welcome.

Their choices carry the story to its ending. If the table wants another
chapter, we can talk about what comes next.
