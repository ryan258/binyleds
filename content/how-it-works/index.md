## 1. Tell me about your gathering

Bring a rough guest count, a possible date, and the general location. Tell
me what brings your guests together, what they enjoy, and how familiar they
are with tabletop games.

We can talk privately about the tone of the story, content to leave out, and
practical things that would help people take part. The
[host’s checklist]({{< relref "host-guide" >}}) has details you can bring along.

If it helps, bring one idea for the kind of story your guests would enjoy.
Friends trying their first game might like a gentle mystery. Guests who love
escape rooms might enjoy a trail of clues. A group that already plays might
want a familiar world and a new decision to make.

An inquiry starts the conversation. A reservation requires direct agreement,
and sending a request creates no payment obligation.

## 2. Agree on the evening

I’ll discuss what fits your group, the room, and your schedule. Before you
decide, I’ll confirm timing, what’s included, the quote, and any applicable
terms. If the date or format won’t work, I’ll tell you directly.

Once we’ve agreed, I prepare the story, a first choice that guests can understand
quickly, and any characters or materials in the scope.

## 3. Take a seat with your guests

I set the opening scene, ask what your characters do, and explain the rules as
they come up. You can play alongside your guests while I guide the story and
make room for questions or breaks.

The guests’ choices carry the story to its ending. If the group would like
another chapter, we can talk about what would fit.
