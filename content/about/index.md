## A private gathering first

Every commission is approached as a private gathering first and a tabletop game
second. The story matters. So do the practical conditions that allow people to
enter it comfortably.

That means understanding the guests, reading the room, explaining without
condescension, adapting without losing direction, and knowing when to let the
table take control.

## Quiet command

A guided evening should feel held without feeling controlled. Preparation makes
that possible. When the practitioner understands the story, the likely points of
friction, and the needs of the table, rules can stay light and attention can
remain on the people.

Confidence is expressed through clarity:

- Guests know what is happening and what they can do.
- The host knows what has been agreed and what to expect.
- Boundaries and practical needs are treated as part of good preparation.
- The pace can change without the evening losing its shape.

## Living imagination

Fantasy should not be wallpaper. It becomes meaningful when it gives people an
interesting choice, reveals something about the table, or makes an ordinary
interaction suddenly memorable.

The aim is not to recite a world at the guests. It is to offer a world that
listens back.

## About factual claims

This website intentionally does not publish an unverified biography, years of
experience, awards, client logos, or testimonials. Those details should only
appear when the owner has confirmed them and the people quoted have approved
their use.

The work should earn trust first through the clarity of the process, the care in
the questions, and the honesty of what is - and is not yet - promised.

## The standard

Polished enough for a discerning home. Imaginative enough for favorite friends.
Every decision should support both halves of that sentence.
