## Prepare around the people

I ask what brings everyone together, how much tabletop experience they have,
and what might help them feel comfortable. That can shape the story, the way
I explain a choice, and how much time I leave for questions.

## Keep the next step clear

Before play, I confirm what the host needs to know and what I’ll prepare.
At the table, I describe the situation and give guests a place to begin. When
someone needs a rule explained or a moment to think, I make time for it.

## Give the choices to the table

I prepare a setting with details the guests can act on. They might follow a
clue, question someone unexpected, or make a plan I never saw coming. My job is
to give those choices room to matter.
