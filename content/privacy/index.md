## What the form collects

The consultation form may collect your name, contact details, location, possible
date, expected guest count, tabletop experience, occasion, preferences, and any
comfort or access notes you choose to share.

Do not submit sensitive medical, financial, identity, or security information.
If a private detail becomes relevant later, raise it directly rather than
through the form.

## Why it is used

The information is used to:

- Respond to the consultation request
- Understand whether the proposed gathering is a practical fit
- Prepare for a direct conversation about the evening
- Keep an accurate record of what the host asked for

It should not be sold or used for unrelated marketing without separate consent.

## Where submissions are processed

When this site is hosted on Netlify, the form is processed and stored by
Netlify’s form service. The site owner controls who receives submission
notifications and should configure an appropriate retention practice before
public launch.

## Access, correction, or deletion

After receiving a reply, you can use that same email conversation to ask for a
correction, a copy of the details supplied, or deletion of the consultation
record, subject to any legitimate record-keeping need explained at the time.

## Changes to this note

This note should be reviewed whenever the form provider, data use, notification
recipients, or retention practice changes. The public version must describe the
actual operating practice rather than an aspirational one.
